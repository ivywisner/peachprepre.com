Short Answer
Plan on $205–$520 for the course, and four to eight weeks to get through it part-time. Georgia requires one 75-hour pre-license course from a GREC-approved school, and it's most of the licensing bill — the fixed state fees only add about $345 more. It's also most of the licensing timeline: once your certificate lands, the background report is same-day, PSI results are instant, and the license itself can be issued at the test center.
The Price

Same Curriculum, Very Different Price Tags

Georgia requires one course — the 75-hour salesperson pre-license course — from a GREC-approved school, and that's the entire education requirement. Every approved school teaches to the same state-set curriculum: online packages run from about $205 at the budget end to around $520 for premium bundles with exam prep and instructor support, and schools run sales constantly. Classroom formats at local schools typically cost more and move on the school's schedule instead of yours.

What the extra money buys is polish, support, and study tools — worth paying for if they help you actually finish, but never a different certificate. One thing worth weighing beyond price: the course ends in its own final exam, so a package that actually teaches well beats a cheap one you'll have to supplement later.

The Real Timeline

The Course Is the Whole Timeline

Here's Georgia's quirk, and it's a friendly one: there's almost no bureaucratic purgatory after the course. Most people take four to eight weeks to finish the 75 hours part-time — and then the rest of the process sprints. The GCIC background report is same-day at your county sheriff's office, PSI exam seats are usually available within days, results are instant, and PSI can issue your license at the test center itself (they say to allow 24 hours). In most states you finish the education and then wait on the state; in Georgia, when the course is done, you're the only thing between you and the license.

Which means the honest way to compress the Georgia timeline isn't paperwork tricks — it's finishing the course at a steady pace and walking into PSI prepared enough to pass once. At $175 per attempt with no portion discount, the retake line is where unprepared candidates quietly spend the most.

💡

Time the GCIC report for the end of the course, not the start. The background report must be under 60 days old when you use it — pull it too early and you'll be buying a second one. Grab it in your final course week and it's fresh exactly when you need it. The full timeline guide walks the whole 2–4 month sequence.

Online vs. Classroom

It's (Mostly) All Online Now

Most Georgians now do the entire 75 hours online — the course, its final exam, and the certificate, all from home. Classroom schools still exist and some people learn better in a room, but you pay for the room and drive to it. The only step that must happen in person is the state exam itself, at a PSI test center. If you were searching for a fully online Georgia real estate license: the education half is, the exam half isn't.
