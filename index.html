FAQ

Common Questions About the GA Real Estate Exam

How many questions are on the Georgia real estate exam?
The Georgia real estate salesperson exam, administered by PSI, consists of 152 multiple-choice questions — 100 on the national portion and 52 on the Georgia supplement. The two portions are scored separately, and you must pass both to receive your license.
What score do you need to pass the Georgia real estate salesperson exam?
You need 75% or higher on each portion — the national portion and the Georgia supplement are scored separately, and you must pass both. A strong score on one portion can't make up for a weak score on the other.
Is the Georgia real estate exam hard?
It trips up plenty of first-time test takers — especially the Georgia supplement, which covers GREC license law, BRRETA agency rules, and Georgia-specific practice that generic national study tools barely touch. You have to pass both portions separately at 75%, so you can't lean on national knowledge to carry you. Georgia-specific preparation makes a significant difference.
What's the difference between the national and state portions of the Georgia exam?
The national portion (100 questions) covers general real estate principles — contracts, finance, property ownership, agency, and federal law. The Georgia supplement (52 questions) covers state-specific content: Georgia license law and GREC rules, real estate practice in Georgia including BRRETA, and finance and closing. The state supplement is where most test takers get tripped up.
Is there math on the Georgia real estate exam?
Yes — you can expect problems involving commission calculations, prorations, loan math, and closing costs. The math is straightforward once you understand the formulas; it's not advanced, just unfamiliar if you haven't drilled it.
How many practice questions should I do before the Georgia real estate exam?
There's no magic number, but the goal is consistent exposure across all topic areas until you're scoring confidently — not just memorizing answers. PeachPrep RE tracks your accuracy by topic so you always know which areas still need work before exam day.
What accuracy score should I aim for before taking the GA real estate exam?
We recommend aiming for 85% or higher across all topic areas before scheduling your exam — that's the sweet spot where most test takers feel genuinely confident walking in. Since Georgia requires 75% on both portions, anything consistently above 75% is a good sign you're on track. PeachPrep RE tracks your accuracy by topic so you can see exactly where you still need work.
Does PeachPrep RE replace the 75-hour pre-license course?
No — Georgia requires a 75-hour salesperson pre-license course, and PeachPrep RE isn't a substitute for it. It's the practice layer on top: the course teaches you the material, and PeachPrep gets you from "finished the course" to "ready for exam day."
